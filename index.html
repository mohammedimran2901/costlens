For the cost accountant · deep dives & efficiency

"Where should I dig first — and what is each line worth?"

The efficiency watchlist ranks a trust's largest service lines by the £ value of closing the gap to the efficient quartile (25th percentile of all submitting trusts), and shows whether the gap is yours or the NHS's: your unit-cost change vs the national movement for the same line.

The example, real: outpatient follow-up (WF01A) at one trust costs £219 per attendance against a p25 of £110 — and its unit cost rose +19% over five years while the NHS average for the same line fell 1%. That one line prices at £16m/yr of headroom. The workspace surfaces the whole list, ranked.

"What is our cost base doing — and what does next year look like?"

The board view shows five years of total submitted cost, a CAGR, and a 2025/26 projection with a quantified standard error — then the lines your board should worry about: services where your costs grew faster than the NHS experienced, each priced in £ per year.

The example, real: a small trust's submitted cost rose from £204m to £267m (+7.0% CAGR, 2020/21→2024/25), projecting to £276.6m ±£5.5m for 2025/26 — with its above-trend service lines already flagged for the board pack.

For the finance team · benchmarking & board packs

"Are we actually expensive — or just different?"

Unit costs vary for three reasons: prices (MFF), casemix, and efficiency. CostLens separates what the published data allows: every comparison is shown against the national median, the efficient quartile, and your size-quartile peers — with submission counts so you know which gaps rest on solid data. Every figure traces to an official published file, vintages disclosed.

Questions finance teams ask us

Is this patient-level or identifiable data?

No. Everything is aggregated published data from NHS England's National Cost Collection — the same source as the national schedule of NHS costs. No patient data, nothing confidential, no IG toolkit approval needed to look at it.

How is this different from Model Health System?

Model Health System shows you the numbers; CostLens decomposes them — national price movement vs your own divergence — prices each gap in £, benchmarks against size-matched peers, and projects forward. It's analysis you can put in a board pack, not a dashboard you have to interpret.

What happens when new data is published?

We ingest each new National Cost Collection within days of publication and re-run every calculation. Your views always reflect the latest vintage, labelled.
