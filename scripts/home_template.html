Why this is different

  • Organisation-level, not just national averages. We ingest the trust-level source files most teams never get to use.
  • Fair comparisons. Peer groups are built by trust size, so a small district hospital isn't benchmarked against a teaching giant.
  • Suppression is respected. Where submissions are suppressed, we say so — never fabricate.
  • Every number is traceable. Built entirely from NHS England's published National Cost Collection, vintages disclosed.
